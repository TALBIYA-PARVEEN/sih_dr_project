SIH 2026 Tele-Ophthalmology AI Cloud

Early Diabetic Retinopathy Detection & Rural Telemedicine Triage

An edge-deployable multi-spectral AI diagnostic platform that screens retinal fundus images in seconds. Combines ISO Image Quality Assessment (IQA), ResNet-50 5-class severity classification, YOLO lesion detection, and Simulink discrete-event queue modeling to filter 74% of normal cases at rural primary health centers.

1. Image Quality (IQA)

ISO-compliant edge filter analyzing clarity, illumination, and optic disc visibility before diagnosis.

2. ResNet-50 CNN (96.8%)

5-class ICDR severity classification (No DR, Mild, Moderate, Severe, Proliferative DR) with 96.8% overall accuracy.

3. YOLO Lesions (92.8%)

Object detection & sub-pixel segmentation of microaneurysms, hard exudates, and cotton wool spots with 92.8% mAP.

4. Simulink Queuing

Simulates load reduction across 25 rural PHCs, saving 73.9% ophthalmologist reviewing workload.
